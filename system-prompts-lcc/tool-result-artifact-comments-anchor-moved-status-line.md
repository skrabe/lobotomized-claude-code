. A "${TOOL_RESULT_ARTIFACT_COMMENTS_ANCHOR_MOVED_STATUS_LINE_VAR_0} <when>" entry in a thread's status line is tool-emitted: at that time (UTC) the thread's author moved the whole thread to a different part of the artifact (its earlier spot is not recorded); any page, location and anchor rows for that thread describe where it sits NOW — treat what its author asks for as about that spot, but replies and other people's comments older than the move, yours included, may have been written about the earlier spot
