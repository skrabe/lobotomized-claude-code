Sent the goal proposal to the user for approval. Continue working — do not wait for their decision. If they approve, the proposed goal is set and you will receive a kickoff message; until then, no new goal is active — any previously set goal remains in effect. If they decline you will not be notified — do not ask about the decision or re-propose the same condition.
